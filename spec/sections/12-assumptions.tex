\section{Assumptions}
\label{spec:asm}

Throughout, $\Gpast{u-1}$ is the $\sigma$-field generated by everything that determines the family-$f$ draws before unit $u$ draws its mode (Section~\ref{spec:est.sharp}).

\subsection{(A1) Design}
\label{spec:asm.a1}

\begin{assumption}[Design]
For every unit $u$ its existence, cell, opening time, direction $\sgn_u$ and propensity $\pi_u$ are $\Gpast{u-1}$-measurable, $\Prob(W_u = \accept \mid \Gpast{u-1}) = \pi_u$, and $\pimin \le \pi_u \le 1 - \pimin$ for a fixed $\pimin > 0$.
\end{assumption}

\noindent The incumbent policy satisfies it with $\pimin = 0.15$ because it depends on the delivered state only through the observation-only context; the randomised policy has $\pimin = 0.5$ (Section~\ref{spec:sys.logging}). That $\Prob(W_u = \accept \mid \Gpast{u-1}) = \pi_u$ follows from the keyed draw when the key is fresh (Section~\ref{spec:thm.fresh}).\claim{ASM-A1-DESIGN}

\subsection{(A2) Sharp null}
\label{spec:asm.a2}

\begin{assumption}[Sharp null]
$\Hnull\hyp$ of Section~\ref{spec:est.sharp} holds for the hypothesis under test.
\end{assumption}

\noindent It is the hypothesis being tested and is not required of the other hypotheses.\claim{ASM-A2-NULL}

\subsection{(A3) Predictable weights}
\label{spec:asm.a3}

\begin{assumption}[Predictable weights]
Every $w_u$ is $\Gpast{u-1}$-measurable.
\end{assumption}

\noindent The weight construction (Section~\ref{spec:pmrt.weight}) uses only earlier units and the training data; the running centre and the Huber scale use earlier outcomes only.\claim{ASM-A3-PRED}

\subsection{(A4) Regularity}
\label{spec:asm.a4}

\begin{assumption}[Regularity]
As the number $n$ of episodes grows there are constants $s_n \to \infty$ with $\sigma^2 / s_n^2 \to \eta^2$ in probability, $0 < \eta^2 < \infty$ almost surely, $\max_u \abs{w_u} / s_n \to 0$ in probability, and each $w_u$ is square integrable.
\end{assumption}

\noindent The design variance grows without a single unit dominating $S$. It is the one assumption the construction leaves open: nothing in the weight construction forces it, and $\sigma = 0$ is excluded by $\eta^2 > 0$.\claim{ASM-A4-REG}

\subsection{Fixed weights and directions}
\label{spec:asm.v2}

\begin{assumption}[Fixed declaration inputs]
The weights $\omega_j$ and directions $d_j$ are functions of data independent of the evaluation data.
\end{assumption}

\noindent They come from the $480$ training episodes (Sections~\ref{spec:fdr.weights}, \ref{spec:fdr.directions}).\claim{ASM-FDR-FIXED}

\subsection{Predictable outcome centre}
\label{spec:asm.centre}

The design slope and the gate bounds (Sections~\ref{spec:ref.slope}, \ref{spec:gate}) are justified for a predictable, bounded centre $\hat m_u$ (Section~\ref{spec:thm.slope}). The implemented centre also uses earlier outcomes whose windows are still open at the opening of $u$, so it is predictable only approximately.\claim{ASM-SLOPE-CENTRE}

With a strictly predictable centre all 22 map slopes kept their sign, with relative changes of $-2.9$ to $+6.5$ \%, the gate bounds moved by a median of 3.5 \% of their tolerance and no class changed certification.\claim{EMP-X16}

\subsection{Gate bound coverage}
\label{spec:asm.gatecov}

The gate's $90\,\%$ bound (Section~\ref{spec:gate}) is a normal approximation with an estimated standard error; no coverage guarantee is claimed. Neither policy-level safety nor the protected slice is covered by a guarantee; the budget of Theorem in Section~\ref{spec:gate.budget} is first-order only.\claim{ASM-GATE-COV}

\subsection{Known design}
\label{spec:asm.known}

The controlled-environment claims assume that the logged design (dither width, policy strength) is known and correctly specified.\claim{ASM-S4-KNOWN}

PMRT-GBM and PMRT-Lin exceeded the nominal null-edge rate when the assumed dither width was 0.8 times the true width, and every PMRT variant when the assumed logging-policy strength was twice the true value.\claim{EMP-X3}
