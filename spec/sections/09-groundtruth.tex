\section{Ground truth}
\label{spec:gt}

\subsection{Knockout labels}
\label{spec:gt.label}

For a hypothesis $\hyp$ let $a_1, \dots, a_n$ be the knockout contrasts $\sgn_u \Delta_u^{r,k}$ of its units (Section~\ref{spec:est.tauaa}), grouped by episode.

\begin{definition}[Knockout label]
The interval $[\ell, \upsilon]$ is the $[2.5, 97.5]$ percentile interval of $4000$ episode-cluster bootstrap draws of the ratio of sums (sum of contrasts over sum of unit counts in the resampled episodes); the generator is seeded by $[6617, f_i, r_i, k_i, o_i]$ with the indices of family, relation, KPI and orientation. Let $\bar a$ be the mean and
\begin{equation*}
\delta_k = \max\bigl(0.05\,\max_h \abs{\bar a_{h,k}},\ \mathrm{floor}_k\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{floor}_{\sfn{pv}} = 0.5,\ \mathrm{floor}_k = 0 \text{ otherwise,}
\end{equation*}
the maximum over all hypotheses $h$ of KPI $k$. The label is, in this order:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textsc{indet}, if there are fewer than $10$ units, fewer than $3$ episodes, or a non-finite value;
\item \textsc{true}, with the sign of $\bar a$, if ($\ell > 0$ or $\upsilon < 0$) and $\abs{\bar a} \ge \delta_k$;
\item \textsc{null}, if $\ell \ge -\delta_k$ and $\upsilon \le \delta_k$;
\item \textsc{indet} otherwise.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\noindent \textsc{indet} hypotheses are unscored.\claim{DEF-GT-LABEL}

\coderef{oran_causal.sim.system.decision.gt:classify, oran_causal.sim.system.decision.gt:cluster_boot}

On the randomised logs the knockout ground truth labels 29 hypotheses true, 21 null and 10 indeterminate.\claim{EMP-GT-LABELS}

With the materiality threshold halved PMRT's precision rose to 1.00 on both kinds of log, and with it doubled it fell to 0.59 and 0.73; halving or doubling $\delta_k$ changed 10 and 16 of the 60 labels, the floor and interval level at most 2.\claim{EMP-GT-SENS}

\subsection{Existence rule}
\label{spec:gt.exist}

The existence rule labels a hypothesis \emph{real} when its bootstrap interval excludes zero, at any magnitude (no threshold $\delta_k$).

Under the existence rule, 26 of PMRT's 28 declarations on the randomised logs and all 22 on the confounded logs were real effects with the declared sign, and its neighbour and far declarations were labelled true in 12 of 13 and 7 of 7 cases.\claim{EMP-GT-EXIST}
