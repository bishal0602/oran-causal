\section{PMRT statistic}
\label{spec:pmrt}

\subsection{Assignment score}
\label{spec:pmrt.score}

\begin{definition}[Assignment score]
For a unit $u$ with logged mode $W_u$ and propensity $\pi_u = \Prob(W_u = \accept \mid \Gpast{u-1})$,
\begin{equation*}
v_u = \sgn_u \bigl( L(W_u) - \pi_u \bigr).
\end{equation*}
\end{definition}

\noindent For a two-mode table $L(W_u) = \Ind\{W_u = \accept\}$, so $\Ex[v_u \mid \Gpast{u-1}] = 0$ and $\Var(v_u \mid \Gpast{u-1}) = \pi_u(1 - \pi_u)$. For a table over modes $m$ with probabilities $\pi_{um}$ and doses $L_m$ (\texttt{LEVEL\_V2}),
\begin{equation*}
v_u = \sgn_u \Bigl( L(W_u) - \sum_m \pi_{um} L_m \Bigr), \qquad
\Var(v_u \mid \Gpast{u-1}) = \sum_m \pi_{um} L_m^2 - \Bigl(\sum_m \pi_{um} L_m\Bigr)^{2},
\end{equation*}
which is $\pi_u(1 - \pi_u)$ for binary doses.\claim{DEF-PMRT-SCORE}

\subsection{Weight construction}
\label{spec:pmrt.weight}

The weight $w_u$ is a $\Gpast{u-1}$-measurable function of the data of earlier units, of the training units, and of the context of $u$. For a hypothesis $\hyp$ the construction has seven steps.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Target bins.} The outcome window is split into the post-opening bins $[0,30)$, $[30,60)$, $[60,90)$, $[90,150)$ s (Section~\ref{spec:sys.hyp}); the kernel weights bins, not seconds. A unit without the fourth bin (the episode ends first) uses the kernel on bins $1$ to $3$.
\item \emph{Receiver slots.} The cells of relation $r$ are mapped to slots: own $1$, neighbours $4$, far $2$. Cells are ranked by the receiver score $\rho_f$ of the family (descending, ties to the lower cell index); the first slots of each group take the top-ranked cells one each, and the last slot holds the sum of the remaining cells.
\item \emph{Ridge adjustment.} On the training units each slot-by-bin outcome $E$ is regressed on covariates $X$ with a ridge penalty chosen by generalised cross-validation over $\lambda/n \in [10^{-3}, 100]$, after demeaning within episode and sign. $X$ holds the pre-opening bins (a missing fourth bin imputed as twice the third), the pre-opening sums of the other KPIs, the pre-opening value of the own cell if $r \neq \sfn{own}$, the unit context, the history of past design-centred modes, $t_0/T_{\mathrm{ep}}$ and a missing-bin flag. The residual $\tilde E$ removes what is predictable from $\Gpast{u-1}$.
\item \emph{Matched filter.} With $\mu = \tilde E^\top v / \sum_u v_u^2$ the effect profile over slot-by-bin cells, $\Sigma$ the Ledoit--Wolf covariance of $\tilde E$ and $p$ the profile length,
\begin{equation*}
\psi = \bigl(\Sigma + 10^{-8}\, \mathrm{tr}(\Sigma)/p\; I\bigr)^{-1} \mu .
\end{equation*}
The \emph{loadsp} kernel replaces $\psi$ by the rank-one product of a same-signed spatial profile and a least-squares time profile fitted to $\mu$; the \emph{plain} kernel uses $\psi$.
\item \emph{Running score centre.} The score residual is centred with the running mean of earlier units of the same sign, shrunk towards the training mean with pseudo-counts $n_0 = 20$ and $n_1 = 50$ (the plain running mean once a sign has $n_1$ units) and with per-sign training intercepts.
\item \emph{Huber clip.} A predicted scale $s_u = \kappa\, e^{h_u/2}$ is formed with $h_u$ the ridge (penalty $30\, n/100$) prediction of $\log(r^2 + 10^{-3}\,\overline{r^2})$ from standardised covariates and $\kappa = \mathrm{median}(\abs{r}/e^{h/2})/0.6745$. The matched-filter response $\hat w_u$ is clipped,
\begin{equation*}
w_u = \clip\bigl(\hat w_u,\; -c\, s_u,\; c\, s_u\bigr), \qquad c = 2.5 .
\end{equation*}
\item \emph{Orientation.} With $z_{\mathrm{train}}$ the plain statistic on the training units, if $\abs{z_{\mathrm{train}}} \ge 2$ the orientation is $o = \sgn(\Ind_{<3}^\top \mu)$, where $\Ind_{<3}$ selects the bins $1$ to $3$; otherwise $o$ is unset and the sign of an edge comes from the plain arm.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent The paper's PMRT is the loadsp arm with the Huber clip (\emph{loadsp\_c}) declared as in Section~\ref{spec:fdr.wby}. The code also computes the plain clipped arm (\emph{plain\_c}) and the larger of the two statistics; these are not the reported method.\claim{DEF-PMRT-WEIGHT}

\subsection{Statistic}
\label{spec:pmrt.stat}

\begin{definition}[PMRT statistic]
For the tested units $\mathcal{U}$ of a hypothesis,
\begin{equation*}
S = \sum_{u \in \mathcal{U}} v_u w_u, \qquad
\sigma^2 = \sum_{u \in \mathcal{U}} \pi_u (1 - \pi_u)\, w_u^2, \qquad
z = S / \sigma .
\end{equation*}
\end{definition}

\noindent \emph{Edge cases.} If $\sigma = 0$ (all weights zero) the code sets $z = 0$ and $p = 1$; the engine divides by $\sigma$ replaced by $1$, where also $S = 0$, with the same outcome. If an outcome is not finite the hypothesis is \emph{undetermined} (\texttt{degenerate\_outcome}) and not tested.\claim{DEF-PMRT-STAT}

\subsection{Support rule}
\label{spec:pmrt.support}

A hypothesis is tested only if its unit table has at least $30$ units, $5$ accepted, $5$ rejected and units from at least $3$ episodes. Otherwise its p-value is NaN (untested): it counts in $m = 60$ and is never declared.\claim{DEF-PMRT-SUPPORT}
