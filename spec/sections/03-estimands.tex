\section{Estimands}
\label{spec:est}

\subsection{Policy-averaged assignment effect}
\label{spec:est.taupi}

\begin{equation*}
\tau_u^{\pi} = \mathbb{E}_{\pi}\bigl[y_u \mid \mathcal{F}_{u-1}, W_u = \text{accept}\bigr]
             - \mathbb{E}_{\pi}\bigl[y_u \mid \mathcal{F}_{u-1}, W_u = \text{reject}\bigr]
\end{equation*}

The policy-averaged assignment effect tau\_pi of hypothesis (f, r, k) is the limit of the mean of sgn\_u tau\_u\^{}pi over the units of f as the number of episodes grows (Definition 1).\claim{DEF-EST-TAUPI}

\subsection{Sharp null}
\label{spec:est.sharp}

The sharp null H0(f, r, k) states that the outcome a unit of f would have at any cell and opening time is the same under any assignment of f's modes; it is stated over keys, not realised units.\claim{DEF-EST-H0}

\subsection{Knockout effect}
\label{spec:est.tauaa}

The knockout effect tau\_AA is the mean of sgn\_u tau\_u\^{}AA, the accept-minus-reject difference of the 90 s post-window KPI sum in rollouts from the copied state with every other unit accepted (Definition 2).\claim{DEF-EST-TAUAA}
