\section{Controlled environments}
\label{spec:env}

A controlled environment has a known causal graph over $d$ variables and a logging design that chooses the action. The worlds are E1 linear graph, E2 rare regime, E3 delayed harm, E4 latent confounding (confounding strength $\lambda \in \{0, 0.5, 1, 1.5\}$) and E5 composed. The \emph{regimes} are R1 independent redraws of the action, R2 a dithered setpoint, R3 a logged confounded policy (E4 only) and R4 unlogged confounding (E4 only). Under R2 the setpoint takes $20$ values within $\pm 25\,\%$ of the range around the midpoint, each held for $n/20$ rows, plus an independent dither of $\pm 10\,\%$ of the range. The sample size runs from $n = 500$ to $24\,000$. The ground truth is the edge set of the graph.\claim{DEF-ENV}

\subsection{Arms}
\label{spec:env.arms}

PMRT-Lin and PMRT-GBM use the PMRT core with the linear and gradient-boosted statistic respectively (the assignment score of Section~\ref{spec:pmrt.score} against a fitted predictable outcome), the Besag--Clifford p-value of Section~\ref{spec:inf.bc} (frozen run records: $B = 9999$, $h = 20$), plain Benjamini--Yekutieli at $q = 0.05$ ($\omega_j \equiv 1$, no prior directions) and no Huber clip. Design-adjusted and design-blind tests (correlation, partial correlation, RCoT, Granger, \dots) are compared against them with the same step-up.\claim{DEF-ENV-ARMS}

\subsection{Validity}
\label{spec:env.validity}

A condition is classified from the null-edge rate $\hat\alpha$ (rate of declared edges among true-null edges) and its $95\,\%$ bootstrap interval ($2000$ resamples by seed): \textsc{invalid} if the lower end exceeds $0.05$; \textsc{valid} if the upper end is at most $0.075$; \textsc{inconclusive} otherwise. The claims are C1 (design-blind tests lose validity under dither), C2a, C2b (design-based and design-adjusted tests stay valid) and C3 (allowance of invalid conditions).

Tests that ignored the design were close to their nominal rate under independent logging but lost it under a dithered setpoint, with null-edge rates of 0.33 to 0.94.\claim{EMP-S4-C1}

The design-based and design-adjusted tests remained near 0.05 under every logging design and all four protocol claims were supported, except that design-adjusted RCoT was invalid and PMRT-Lin exceeded its allowance of invalid conditions under the confounded policy.\claim{EMP-S4-C2C3}

Under the logged confounded policy the null-edge rate of correlation was 1.00 whenever confounding was present while PMRT-GBM stayed between 0.02 and 0.06, and design-adjusted partial correlation also stayed valid.\claim{EMP-S4-E4}

With its threshold calibrated on the placebo column, the discovery stage of CDD O-RAN passed the placebo check but exceeded the nominal null-edge rate in 9 of 30 conditions.\claim{EMP-S4-CDD}

\subsection{Power}
\label{spec:env.power}

In the latent-confounding environment the recall of PMRT-GBM did not exceed 0.075 while design-adjusted partial correlation reached 0.90; on the randomised simulator logs pooled recalls were 0.72, 0.69 and 0.79 for design-adjusted partial correlation, context-only partial correlation and PMRT.\claim{EMP-POWER}
