\section{Protocols}
\label{spec:pro}

For each dataset the analysis rules, seeds and pass criteria were fixed before the evaluation data were generated. The analysis documents (\texttt{FrozenDoc}) are pinned by the SHA-256 of their LF-normalised bytes, the evaluation is released only by the campaign guard \texttt{eval\_authorised}, and the fixed analysis code was run once; one amendment changed the fixed code.\claim{PRO-FROZEN}

\subsection{Seed blocks}
\label{spec:pro.seeds}

Seeds come from disjoint blocks of a registry that is checked for overlaps. The randomised-log study uses the block $188000$--$189999$: development $188000$--$188019$, evaluation $188100$--$188699$, placebo $188700$--$188739$ and knockout ground truth $188800$--$188839$ (units with $t_0 \ge 90$). Records follow the schema \texttt{e6p-disc-rec/1}.\claim{PRO-SEEDS}

\subsection{Tuning data}
\label{spec:pro.tune}

All methods and baseline thresholds were tuned on development episodes separate from the evaluation data; the weight construction is trained on $480$ earlier episodes and $20$ development episodes, and the gate is calibrated on $40$ development episodes (all-accept calibration), which fixes $G_k$ of Section~\ref{spec:gate.tol}.\claim{PRO-TUNE}

\subsection{Post hoc status}
\label{spec:pro.status}

Analyses chosen after a protocol was fixed are reported as post hoc and change no verdict; the gated referee was designed after the result of the ungated referee was known.\claim{PRO-POSTHOC}

\subsection{Randomised logs}
\label{spec:pro.study1}

The protocol criterion comparing PMRT's neighbour-relation F1 with Granger + BY held on only 2 of the 10 smallest subsamples and on 4 of the 5 larger ones, so the discovery verdict on the randomised logs was partial.\claim{EMP-S1-P2}

\subsection{Referee with the gate}
\label{spec:pro.study3}

With the gate, the PMRT referee met every guard but its outcomes were identical to never-sleep on every seed, so its gain over that rule was zero ($R = 0.26$, RLF ratio 0.96).\claim{EMP-S3-GATED}

\subsection{Replication of the ungated referee}
\label{spec:pro.x15}

On the gated dataset's seeds the ungated PMRT referee no longer outperformed never-sleep ($-0.06$ at an RLF ratio of 1.05); pooled over both seed sets its gain was 0.10 with an interval that barely excludes zero, at an RLF ratio of 1.18.\claim{EMP-X15}
