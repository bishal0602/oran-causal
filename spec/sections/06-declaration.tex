\section{Declaration}
\label{spec:fdr}

\subsection{Weighted Benjamini--Yekutieli step-up}
\label{spec:fdr.wby}

The 60 hypotheses of one analysis are declared together by a weighted Benjamini-Yekutieli step-up at q = 0.05, with Roeder-Wasserman weights (floor 0.2, cap 5, mean 1) and one-sided p-values in the prior direction where |z\_prior| $\ge$ 3.\claim{DEF-FDR-WBY}

\subsection{Prior weights}
\label{spec:fdr.weights}

Roeder-Wasserman weights for weighted multiple testing.\claim{CIT-RW}

\subsection{Prior directions}
\label{spec:fdr.directions}

Direction $d_j$: the sign of $z$ on the training episodes where $|z| \ge 3$.

\subsection{Untested hypotheses}
\label{spec:fdr.untested}

Untested hypotheses count in $m$ and are never declared, which is conservative.
