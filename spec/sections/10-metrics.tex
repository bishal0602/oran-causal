\section{Metrics and validity rules}
\label{spec:met}

\subsection{Map metrics}
\label{spec:met.map}

A map is a set of declared edges, each with a sign. Compare it with the knockout labels of Section~\ref{spec:gt.label}; hypotheses labelled \textsc{indet} are excluded from the reference. Let $\mathrm{tp}$ be the declared hypotheses labelled true, $\mathrm{fp}$ those labelled null, $\mathrm{fn}$ the true hypotheses not declared, and $\mathrm{sign\_ok}$ the true positives with the correct sign. A declaration on an \textsc{indet} hypothesis counts in neither $\mathrm{tp}$ nor $\mathrm{fp}$.
\begin{equation*}
\text{precision} = \frac{\mathrm{tp}}{\mathrm{tp} + \mathrm{fp}}, \quad
\text{recall} = \frac{\mathrm{tp}}{\mathrm{tp} + \mathrm{fn}}, \quad
F_1 = \frac{2\,\mathrm{tp}}{2\,\mathrm{tp} + \mathrm{fp} + \mathrm{fn}}, \quad
\text{sign accuracy} = \frac{\mathrm{sign\_ok}}{\mathrm{tp}},
\end{equation*}
with $F_1 = 0$ if $\mathrm{tp} = 0$ and $\mathrm{tp} + \mathrm{fp} + \mathrm{fn} > 0$. The number of declared edges reported (28 in Study 1, 22 in Study 2) includes declarations on \textsc{indet} hypotheses. In Study 1 the 28 are 23 true (all with the correct sign), 2 null and 3 \textsc{indet} (\texttt{ptx|far|pv}, \texttt{sleep|own|pv}, \texttt{sleep|own|v}), so precision is $23/25 = 0.92$ and recall $23/29$; in Study 2 the 22 are 18 true, 3 null and 1 \textsc{indet}, so precision is $18/21 = 0.86$ and recall $18/29$.\claim{DEF-MET-MAP}

\coderef{oran_causal.core.scoring:score_method, oran_causal.core.scoring:confusion}

On the randomised logs PMRT declared 28 edges, with precision 0.92, recall 0.79 and no wrong sign.\claim{EMP-S1-MAP}

On the confounded logs PMRT declared 22 edges, with precision 0.86, recall 0.62 and no wrong sign.\claim{EMP-S2-MAP}

PMRT declared the four edges of the conflict chain with the ground-truth sign on both kinds of log and in every larger subsample of the randomised logs, and 9 of the 10 true neighbour-relation edges, missing only pico sleep to neighbour RLFs.\claim{EMP-S1-CHAIN}

The baselines declared edges on placebo logs, up to 13 on the randomised and up to 29 on the confounded logs; Granger + BY, the strongest, reached a recall of 0.55 with six wrong signs on each kind of log, and placebo-calibrated thresholds cut recall to at most 0.21.\claim{EMP-BASE-MAPS}

\subsection{Decision metrics}
\label{spec:met.decision}

For a referee, $V = 3600\,\cdot\,\mathrm{prot\_viol}/\mathrm{prot\_ue\_s}$, the protected-slice violation rate. Let $\VAA$ be its value under accept-all (\texttt{noarb}) and $\Vref = \min$ of $V$ over the single-xApp references (sub:ES, sub:PowerES, sub:SliceGuarantee).

\begin{definition}[Recovery and eligibility]
\begin{equation*}
R = \frac{\VAA - V}{\VAA - \Vref}, \qquad
\text{retention} = \frac{E_{\mathrm{freeze}} - E}{E_{\mathrm{freeze}} - E_{A}},
\end{equation*}
with $E$ the energy of the referee, $E_{\mathrm{freeze}}$ that of the frozen configuration and $E_A$ that of sub:ES with sub:PowerES. A referee is \emph{eligible} iff $\text{retention} \ge 0.90$ and the four guard ratios to accept-all (service violations, non-protected eMBB violations, low-latency violations, RLF) are at most $1.10$. The scored recovery is $R^* = R$ if eligible and $R^* = \min(R, 0)$ otherwise.
\end{definition}

\noindent Intervals are $90\,\%$ percentile intervals ($5$th and $95$th percentiles) of $10\,000$ bootstrap resamples of seeds, paired across referees, seeded by $[6624, 20, n_{\mathrm{seeds}}]$; eligibility is held at its point value inside the bootstrap. Rates use $95\,\%$ cluster-bootstrap intervals.\claim{DEF-MET-DEC}

\coderef{oran_causal.analysis.system.stats:gate_a}

Without the gate, the PMRT referee reached the highest recovery of any referee, $R = 0.47$, but its RLF ratio of 1.31 (90 \% CI 1.10 to 1.56) breached the 1.10 guard, so it was ineligible; its gain over never-sleep was +0.25 [0.14, 0.36].\claim{EMP-S2-REFEREE}

The breach came from rejected transmit-power requests: PMRT's confounded map lacked the transmit-power to neighbour RLF edge (primary $p = 0.49$), and its referee rejected 8,674 power-up units against the oracle map's 2,370.\claim{EMP-S2-MECH}

Referees using the associational maps raised protected-slice violations to 4.5 to 5.4 times the accept-all level, and several stayed eligible.\claim{EMP-S2-ASSOC}

No gated referee improved on never-sleep: the oracle map differed from it by $-0.04$ (90 \% CI $-0.10$ to $0.03$) and gained only in energy retention, 1.29 against 1.15.\claim{EMP-S3-ORACLE}

A referee that rejected requests at random, at the rate of the ungated referee, had negative recovery.\claim{EMP-X4}

\subsection{Eligibility}
\label{spec:met.eligible}

Mitigation chooses the referee that minimises the protected-slice violations $V$ subject to eligibility as defined above: an energy retention of at least $0.90$ and four guard ratios to accept-all of at most $1.10$.\claim{DEF-SYS-MITIG}

\subsection{Validity checks}
\label{spec:met.validity}

A placebo log applies accept to every request while drawing and logging the modes (Section~\ref{spec:sys.logging}); its hypotheses are all null. A run \emph{passes} the placebo check if nothing is declared and the number of p-values at or below $0.05$ among the $60$ has a binomial tail probability (with success probability $0.05$) of at least the protocol limit $0.01$.

On the randomised placebo logs PMRT declared nothing; 7 of the 60 p-values were at or below 0.05 where 3 were expected, with a binomial tail probability of 0.03 above the protocol limit of 0.01.\claim{EMP-S1-PLACEBO}

On the confounded placebo logs PMRT declared nothing, with 0 of 60 p-values at or below 0.05.\claim{EMP-S2-PLACEBO}

On null outcomes PMRT declared nothing, with p-value rates at or below 0.05 of 0.053 (families 0.047--0.060) on the randomised and 0.050 (0.038--0.063) on the confounded logs, using 999 redraws.\claim{EMP-S1-NULLOUT}

Repeated with 9,999 redraws on 590 null-outcome variants of the randomised logs, declarations occurred in 2 variants, within the limit of 7.5 \%, and the shares of p-values at or below $10^{-3}$ and $2 \cdot 10^{-4}$ were $7.9 \cdot 10^{-4}$ and $1.1 \cdot 10^{-4}$; the evidence rests on ten logged designs.\claim{EMP-S1-STRICT}

Over the 21 hypotheses that the ground truth labels null, the rate of p-values at or below 0.05 was 0.15 (0.048--0.29); over the 12 whose knockout interval also contains zero it was 0.075 (0.017--0.16).\claim{EMP-S1-SMALLNULL}
