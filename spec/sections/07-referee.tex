\section{Design slope and referee}
\label{spec:ref}

\subsection{Design slope}
\label{spec:ref.slope}

\begin{definition}[Design slope]
Let $e_u = y_u - \hat m_u$ with $\hat m_u$ the predictable outcome centre. For the units $\mathcal{U}$ of a class,
\begin{equation*}
\hat\beta = \frac{\sum_u v_u e_u}{\sum_u v_u^2}, \qquad
\mathrm{se} = \frac{\bigl(\sum_u v_u^2 (e_u - \hat\beta v_u)^2\bigr)^{1/2}}{\sum_u v_u^2}.
\end{equation*}
With fewer than $2$ units or $\sum_u v_u^2 = 0$, $\hat\beta = 0$ and $\mathrm{se}$ is missing.
\end{definition}

\noindent The centre $\hat m_u$ is the running mean of the (episode, sign) stratum of earlier units, shrunk with $K = 20$ pseudo-units towards the pooled same-sign mean, plus the pooled within-stratum pre-opening slope $b$ times the pre-opening value. The first unit has $e_u = 0$. The centre uses earlier outcomes, including units whose window is still open (Section~\ref{spec:asm.centre}).

The Horvitz--Thompson estimator
\begin{equation*}
\hat\tau^{\mathrm{HT}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_u \sgn_u \Bigl(\frac{\Ind\{W_u = \accept\}\, e_u}{\pi_u} - \frac{\Ind\{W_u = \reject\}\, e_u}{1 - \pi_u}\Bigr)
\end{equation*}
equals $\hat\beta$ only at $\pi_u = 1/2$ (Lean \texttt{half\_propensity\_identity}); for general $\pi_u$ the slope weights by $\pi_u(1 - \pi_u)$ (Section~\ref{spec:thm.slope}).\claim{DEF-SLOPE}

\subsection{Referee rules}
\label{spec:ref.rules}

The map-driven referee (\texttt{MapGateV2}) decides each request from the map. Write $\hat\beta(f, r, k)$ for the signed map effect and $d = \sgn_u$; let $D_k = d \sum_r \hat\beta(f, r, k)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Effects.} Let $k^*$ be the first of (pv, v, rlf) with $D_k \neq 0$. The request is \emph{harmful} iff $D_{k^*} > 0$ and \emph{costly} iff $D_e > 0$.
\item \emph{Harm relations.} Only the relations own and nbr with $d\,\hat\beta > 0$ count.
\item \emph{Pressure.} From the context: \texttt{own\_prot\_below\_frac} for own, \texttt{nbr\_max\_prot\_below\_frac} for nbr.
\item \emph{Rejection.} Reject iff harmful and (costly or $\mathrm{pressure} > \theta$), $\theta = 0.05$.
\item \emph{Guard conflict.} Let $g$ be the first of (v, rlf) with $D_g \neq 0$. The request is in \emph{conflict} iff harmful and $D_g < 0$. A conflicted rejection is released if the previous unit of the same (cell, family) was also rejected ($K_{\mathrm{conf}} = 1$).
\end{enumerate}
Opening follows the feasible rule of Section~\ref{spec:sys.unit}. The constants are the same for every map. In the gated variant a rejection of a class applies only if the class is certified (Section~\ref{spec:gate}).\claim{DEF-REF-RULES}
